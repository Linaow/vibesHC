The Foundations of Motion
01

I. Law of Inertia

Objects are lazy. They want to keep doing exactly what they are already doing unless a force forces them to change.

∑F = 0 → Δv = 0
02

II. Law of Acceleration

Force is the product of mass and acceleration. To move something heavy quickly, you need a lot of "oomph".

F = m × a
03

III. Action & Reaction

You can't touch something without it touching you back just as hard. Nature is perfectly balanced.

FA = -FB

Derived Truths

Universal Gravitation

The invisible thread connecting apples to planets.

F = G(m₁m₂/r²)

Momentum

The "unstoppability" of an object in motion.

p = m × v

Centripetal Force

The center-seeking force that keeps orbits circular.

F = mv²/r

Kinetic Energy

The energy an object possesses due to its motion, derived from Work and Law II.

KE = ½mv²

The Modern Fingerprint

Newton didn't just discover gravity; he invented a way to predict the future. By turning physical movement into geometry and calculus, he allowed us to build bridges that don't fall, engines that power nations, and rockets that reach the stars. Modern science is simply a series of footnotes to Newton's Principia.
